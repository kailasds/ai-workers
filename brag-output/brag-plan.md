# /brag plan — TCS AI Worker Platform

## Rubric
- **What is it:** An operating system for AI Workers where the Workers learn while doing real modernization work (TIBCO BusinessWorks → Spring Boot), and only learning that survives evidence and governance becomes shared organizational knowledge.
- **Who / what for:** Enterprise modernization and platform teams who run fleets of AI Workers and need to trust what those Workers "know."
- **What sets it apart:** Learning isn't automatically trusted. Independent Workers corroborate it, Sentinel reviews the literal evidence, and only then is it certified, packed and reused by every Worker. Maturity (Silver → Gold → Platinum) is earned from evidence, not activity.
- **Most impressive claim (real data):** "JMS acknowledgement mode CLIENT_ACKNOWLEDGE maps to Spring Boot's manual-acknowledgement container mode." — 6 observations · 3 Workers · 6 runs, 0 contradicting → Ready to Certify.
- **Visual hook:** A real Candidate Knowledge card dropping onto the dark teal shell: "Your AI Workers learn on the job."
- **Real UI shown:** Candidate Decisions card, Learning Landscape graph with stage headers, Sentinel overview, Candidate review → Accept → "now certified", Registry card + "Why Gold?" popover.
- **Tone:** `polished` with `default`'s pace — serious enterprise product, punchy cuts, soft transitions.
- **Share caption:** "Our AI Workers learn on the job. Sentinel decides what becomes knowledge."

## Angle
The product in use, told as the life of one real learning: **observed → corroborated → reviewed → certified → reused.** The viewer follows one claim through the loop.

## Visual identity
Dark teal shell `#0F2A2E → #0C2426`, orange accent `#F2662A`, white 16px-radius cards, Inter 400–800. Captions white on teal; UI shown as real captured components.
